## First viewport

An evergreen horizontal masthead contains Golf Model and visible Markets / My card / Results links. Below it, the event name, course, data timestamp and Upcoming / In play context form one row. A market tab strip exposes 72 holes, 18 holes, Groups and Other markets. Search, book and edge controls sit directly above the market table. A right-hand evidence column links market history and shows honest coverage. No models, deployment vocabulary or research tools interrupt this flow.

When no lines exist, the board says so and gives event research and results as useful destinations. It never fills the table with invented opportunities. With rows, selecting a matchup opens an inline comparison and the frozen model probability/price/EV, then saves that exact quote to a personal card. The card preserves its original timestamp and warns when the quote is no longer current. Saving is research, not bet placement.
