# Matchup desk delivery — October 13, 2026

## Published scope

The live site now uses a new matchup desk at `/`, a browser-local saved quote card at `/card`, and read-only market evidence at `/evidence`. Primary markets are 72-hole, 18-hole, group and other markets. Existing research, comparison, results, lab and system routes remain available in More tools.

A saved selection preserves its original event, market, round/group identity, book, price, model probability, EV, reason and timestamps. Saving does not place a wager. Stale, live, shadow/research, invalid-probability, invalid-price, and incomplete round/group records are not eligible for saving. Unspecified-duration matchups stay in Other markets.

Results shows the available source record, separating wins, losses, pushes, voids and unresolved records. Flat-stake ROI normalizes each recorded profit by its own stored stake; it is not account ROI. Calibration compares original forecasts with observed outcomes and reports sample limitations. These reports refresh as existing results are collected; no training, retuning or model promotion was added.
